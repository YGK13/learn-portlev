## 1. ICP, jobs to be done, trigger events

**Primary ICP (from `growth-os/state/ICP-profile.md`):** a mid-career enterprise executive, most often a CHRO or VP People and adjacent leaders (COO, CIO, Chief of Staff), who is late to AI, has dabbled and is quietly anxious about obsolescence. Evidence anchors the swarm has verified: 54% of organizations have adopted no AI in HR while 92% of CHROs expect further AI integration (SHRM); only 27% of CxOs have a comprehensive AI strategy (Gartner); 60% of C-suite executives say their company plans to lay off employees who cannot or will not use AI (WRITER x Workplace Intelligence); only 3% of organizations say their leaders are highly prepared to manage AI-enabled work (ManpowerGroup).

**Three buyer tiers for the program (from `COURSE_STRATEGY.md`):** Tier 1 CEO/CFO, Tier 2 CHROs with an AI mandate, Tier 3 Chiefs of Staff.

**Jobs to be done**
1. "Answer the board's AI question credibly before someone else does." (status, seat protection)
2. "Deliver a defensible AI baseline in 60 days without a data or governance incident." (risk)
3. "Turn what I know into a fractional book of business at $8K-15K a month per client." (income, optionality)
4. "Stop being the executive who is told to learn AI; become the one who leads it." (identity)

**Trigger events (buy now)**
- A board or CEO asks "what is our AI story?" (the H1 on `/program` is built on this line).
- A new CAIO/Head of AI is hired *elsewhere* in the org and HR is not in the room.
- A vendor AI pilot fails or leaks data; someone must own governance.
- A restructuring memo cites AI; the exec starts a fractional plan B.
- Finishing the free Fractional CAIO Playbook track (5 lessons) and hitting the program CTA.

## 2. Positioning and messaging hierarchy

**One sentence:** For senior operators who already use AI, PortLev Academy is the free curriculum and the $2,500 Fractional CAIO Program that turn you into the executive who owns your company's AI answer, taught by a 3x CHRO who trains frontier models for OpenAI, Meta and Microsoft.

**Three pillars**
1. **Operator, not theorist.** 3x CHRO, 17 builds live in public (13 products and research platforms plus 4 client sites), 2,300+ clients coached, trainer of frontier models for OpenAI, Meta and Microsoft *(repo: /about, /program, /book)*.
2. **Artifacts, not literacy.** Eight modules, eight working instruments (board one-pager, 60-day baseline plan, AI-use policy and RACI, build-buy-platform matrix, ROI model, pilot SOW, internal-authority playbook, retainer kit) *(repo)*.
3. **Free teaches the map, paid is the expedition.** 10 tracks, 42 published lessons, MIT code, CC-BY content *(repo, 2026-09-01 count)*; the programs gate transformation (onboarding, accountability, live access), never information (`STRATEGY.md`).

**Messaging hierarchy (top to bottom of every surface)**
1. Outcome: "Become the executive who owns the AI answer."
2. Proof: the four verified numbers, then the instructor block.
3. Offer: $2,500, self-paced, application-based, 8 artifacts, personally onboarded.
4. Distinction: self-paced program (already building, wants the seat) vs live cohort (learning to build), same price, different stage.
5. Risk reversal: no checkout, no card up front, fit review within two business days, honest "not for you if".
6. Free on-ramp: the CAIO Playbook track, the Brief.

**Voice rules (house):** no em dashes, no Oxford comma, no emoji in headers, direct and opinionated, every number sourced.

## 7. The cross-funnel: learn <-> portlev <-> newsletter

```
portlev.com (B2B hub)  ──"Academy" / "CAIO Program" links──►  learn.portlev.com/program
      ▲                                                              │
      │ "A PortLev build" footer strip, Organization JSON-LD         │ every lesson: Brief capture (source=lesson-<slug>)
      │                                                              │ CAIO Playbook lessons: program CTA
      │                                                              ▼
The Leverage Brief (Beehiiv) ◄──── capture forms on home, lessons, /book, /brief ────  learn.portlev.com
      │
      └── every Monday: one lesson excerpt + permanent /program footer slot; dedicated issues at T0 and T+21
```

**Rules that make it a loop, not three silos**
1. portlev.com links to `/program` (not `/caio-course`) and to `/learn`; learn.portlev.com links back through the nav, the footer strip and Organization/Person JSON-LD (`sameAs`).
2. Every capture form on learn carries a `source` tag; Beehiiv automations segment by it. Program applicants (`caio-course-apply-*`) get a 3-email fit sequence; lesson subscribers get the weekly Brief; book-waitlist subscribers get the launch sequence.
3. The Brief is the retention channel for the free tier and the re-marketing channel for the program: one program mention in every issue's footer, a full issue at most once every three weeks.
4. `llms.txt` on learn names portlev.com as publisher and yurikruman.com as author, so answer engines resolve the three domains to one entity.
5. Cohort alumni (ForwardShare) are the highest-intent program audience; program alumni are the highest-intent fractional-retainer leads for portlev.com.

## 8. Growth loops and retention

- **Content loop:** growth-os drafts a lesson daily from verified intel -> Yuri publishes -> the lesson carries Course/Article schema and an OG card -> it earns a citation or a ranking -> the lesson's capture form adds Brief subscribers -> the Brief sends them back to the next lesson.
- **Artifact loop:** each program participant produces eight artifacts for their own company -> with consent, one redacted artifact becomes a LinkedIn post and a Brief issue -> proof for the next buyer.
- **Alumni loop:** cohort graduates are told "further along?" -> program; program graduates become fractional-retainer referrals for portlev.com.
- **Retention:** the Brief's weekly cadence, the interactive lesson components (KnowledgeCheck, ActionChecklist, TryIt fire analytics events), and, after the first cohort run, the Leverage Lab membership with monthly office hours.
