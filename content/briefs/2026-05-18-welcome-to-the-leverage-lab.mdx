Every week, the **Leverage Brief** delivers one actionable idea for building with AI — no hype, no theory, no 47-item listicles. One idea. One implementation path. Something you can act on this week.

This is the first issue. Let me tell you exactly what you are signing up for.

## Who this is for

The Leverage Brief is for professionals who are done watching the AI wave from the sideline. CHROs who want to deploy AI before their board asks them why they have not. Consultants and coaches who want to 3x their output without 3x-ing their hours. Mid-career executives who can see the AI Wage Gap opening in front of them and intend to be on the right side of it.

If you are here for AI news and trend summaries, there are better newsletters for that. This one is for people who want to build things that generate income, save time and create leverage — and who have enough work experience to implement what they learn.

## What you will get

**52 issues per year.** Each one covers one idea across three layers:

1. **The concept**: what it is and why it matters — in two to three paragraphs, not twenty.
2. **The implementation**: how to actually build it, with a prompt, a workflow or a step-by-step process you can follow.
3. **The signal**: one thing I am watching, learning or testing that you should know about.

No issue will take longer than seven minutes to read. The implementation section will take longer to execute — that is the point.

## The bigger picture

The Leverage Brief is one layer of the PortLev Academy. The free lessons at [learn.portlev.com](/) give you the foundation. The brief gives you a weekly signal and implementation. The [Leverage Lab community](/cohort) is where members go deeper together — sharing results, getting feedback and accelerating each other's progress.

Everything here is free. The only thing you pay for is your time, and the goal of everything I publish is to make that time return ten times its cost.
