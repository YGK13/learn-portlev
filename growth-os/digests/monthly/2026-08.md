## What the swarm learned this month (with sources)

1. **Governance-from-day-one is now the field's practitioner consensus for why agent pilots die, and it has hard numbers.** Only 11-14% of enterprise AI agent pilots reach production at scale; the gap is organizational (scoping, governance, named ownership), not technical. Source: Tricentis, 2026, https://www.tricentis.com/blog/agentic-ai-enterprise.

2. **That failure pattern has a real, current, named example.** Cognizant stood up a dedicated EMEA AI unit specifically because enterprise agent pilots are failing at scale. Source: 2026-07-28, https://www.techtimes.com/articles/321781/20260728/cognizant-launches-emea-ai-unit-enterprise-agent-pilots-fail-scale.html.

3. **The EU AI Act's transparency and enforcement obligations went live the same day this month's scan ran.** As of 2026-08-02: Article 50 chatbot-disclosure, AI-content-marking and deepfake-labeling duties, plus the European Commission's full GPAI enforcement toolkit, are binding law. Standalone high-risk obligations (hiring tools) are pushed to 2027-12-02. Penalties remain up to EUR 15 million or 3% of global turnover. Source: https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/eu-ai-act-august-2026-transparency-obligations-agency-checklist.

4. **US state AI-employment law has moved into a second generation: audit, reporting and affirmative anti-discrimination duties, not just disclosure.** Illinois HB 3773 (effective 2026-01-01), Colorado's live employer-obligation framework (impact assessments, worker notice, appeal right, public disclosure), California's FEHA/Automated Decision Systems rules in active 2026 enforcement. Source: Ius Laboris, 2026-07-30, https://iuslaboris.com/insights/ai-regulation-in-the-us-the-new-state-laws-employers-cant-ignore/.

5. **The belief-practice gap inside HR is now measured directly, and it matches this ICP's lived experience closer than any prior stat in the profile.** 54% of organizations have adopted no AI in HR and have no 2026 plans to, even as 92% of CHROs themselves expect further AI integration. Source: SHRM, "The State of AI in HR 2026," n=1,722, https://www.shrm.org/topics-tools/research/state-of-ai-hr-2026/full-report. This is now cited in `ICP-profile.md`'s "Paralyzed by where to start" anchor, added this run.
