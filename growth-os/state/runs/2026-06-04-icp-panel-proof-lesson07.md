It names her specific fear (losing the seat, being routed around) in her own words, refuses to lecture her, and hands her one concrete sub-hour move on her actual work this week. That is exactly the rung-1-to-2 job: remove the shame of being late and show the gap is one focused hour, not years. Under the decision rule that is a clean pass.

The correct response is not to bolt monetization onto this lesson but to route him to portfolio and money-OS content where he belongs.

The fix is a clear handoff to deployment-at-scale content, which the closing link to the AI Operating Model lesson begins but should make explicit for an already-deploying reader.
