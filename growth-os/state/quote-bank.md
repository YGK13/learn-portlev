# PortLev Academy Quote Bank

Verified pullquotes for learn.portlev.com lessons. Every quote below is copied verbatim from a named owned source: the manuscript of "Closing the AI Wage Gap" or the AI Wage Gap Podcast transcripts (Alon Bochman, Kim Pecina and Yuri Kruman). Nothing here is paraphrased, merged or invented.

Two sources are in play:
- "Closing the AI Wage Gap" is the book manuscript. Chapter is noted when clearly identifiable.
- "AI Wage Gap Podcast" covers the two interview transcripts. Speaker attribution matches who actually said the line.

Topic coverage spans all ten curriculum tracks: how AI works, prompting, AI workflows, AI agents, governance and risk, personal brand and website, building agents hands-on, leading AI adoption, the AI Wage Gap and first steps and anxiety.
