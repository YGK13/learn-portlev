## 2. The gap

No mainstream source (HBR, McKinsey, Fortune) has staked a direct claim on the "will AI take my CHRO seat" angle. The existing content falls into three buckets:

1. **"Not replacement, transformation"** — the BCG/IMD/Gartner consensus. Correct but unsatisfying to an anxious CHRO who already knows this and still has the fear.
2. **Governance/compliance-first** — heavy emphasis on data risk and AI governance. Useful but misses the career-anxiety angle.

None directly name the conviction-anxiety gap (believing AI is essential while delaying personal commitment due to job-security fear) or give a CHRO a specific week-one move.
