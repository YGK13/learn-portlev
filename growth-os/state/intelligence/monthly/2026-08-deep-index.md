## Sweep 4 — News and Regulation

22. **EU AI Act: what actually takes effect 2026-08-02 versus what got delayed.** Confirmed via direct fetch, article published 2026-07-18. LIVE on August 2, 2026: Article 50 transparency duties (chatbot disclosure "perceivable in the interaction itself," machine-readable marking of AI-generated content, deepfake labeling, AI-generated-text disclosure on public-interest matters with an editorial exemption) and the European Commission's full GPAI enforcement toolkit (information requests, model access rights, recall powers), since underlying GPAI obligations (Articles 51-56) already started 2025-08-02. DELAYED by the 2026-06-16 Parliament amendments: standalone high-risk system obligations (Annex III, e.g. hiring tools) pushed to 2027-12-02; product-embedded high-risk systems (Annex I) pushed to 2028-08-02. Penalties remain up to EUR 15 million or 3% of global turnover. This is the single most concrete, dated regulatory fact for any US-based mid-market or Series C+ executive who touches EU users. Rung 4-5. Domain: regulation. Strength: signal, HIGH.
   Source: https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/eu-ai-act-august-2026-transparency-obligations-agency-checklist

23. **US state AI-employment law has moved into a second generation: audit, reporting and affirmative anti-discrimination duties, not just disclosure.** Ius Laboris, published 2026-07-30. Illinois HB 3773 (effective 2026-01-01) amends the Illinois Human Rights Act to prohibit AI that has the effect of discriminating against employees or applicants across the full employment lifecycle. Colorado's employer-obligation framework (impact assessments, worker notice when AI drives an employment decision, an appeal right, and a public disclosure of AI systems in use) is now live. California's amended FEHA/Automated Decision Systems rules (effective 2025-10) continue into 2026 enforcement. Direct, current compliance-obligation content for the ai-governance track's "AI regulation for leaders" lesson. Rung 4-5. Domain: regulation. Strength: signal, HIGH.
   Source: https://iuslaboris.com/insights/ai-regulation-in-the-us-the-new-state-laws-employers-cant-ignore/

24. **Federal-vs-state AI regulation fight remains unresolved and directly affects which state rules an employer must actually plan around.** The executive order establishing a DOJ AI Litigation Task Force (active since 2026-01-10) to challenge state AI laws in federal court, and conditioning $42B in BEAD broadband funding on states repealing "onerous" AI laws, has NOT preempted state laws to date; a moratorium was stripped from the 2025 budget reconciliation bill on a 99-1 Senate vote. Practical takeaway for the ICP: Colorado's and Illinois' obligations (item 23) are live now regardless of the federal fight, so "wait for federal clarity" is not a defensible compliance posture. Rung 4-5. Domain: regulation. Strength: signal.
   Source: https://www.whitecase.com/insight-alert/state-ai-laws-under-federal-scrutiny-key-takeaways-executive-order-establishing
