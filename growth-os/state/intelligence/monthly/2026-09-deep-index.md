## Sweep 2: Tips and Tactics (4 items)

1. **"Skill collecting" is a trap: curate your AI skill stack, do not accumulate it.** An AI "skill" is an instruction file the agent reads, not an app for humans, and the description field is the single most important part of it. Warns against the "Pokemon-card trap" of collecting skills faster than auditing them, which degrades agent performance. Source: AI News and Strategy Daily with Nate B. Jones, "Agent Skills: How to Test One Before You Keep It," 2026-08-01, https://shows.acast.com/ai-news-strategy-daily-with-nate-b-jones/episodes/agent-skills-how-to-test-one-before-you-keep-it. ICP note: applies directly to any exec building a personal or team AI-skill library, more skills is not more capability past a point. Rung 4, domain prompting, strength signal.

2. **The real skill with AI agents is verification, not prompt-writing.** The bottleneck for productive agent use is not instructing AI well, it is confidently verifying that what it did is correct, and "read every line" is only one of several verification methods. Source: Simon Willison's Weblog, "More than just code review," 2026-08-22, https://simonwillison.net/2026/Aug/22/more-than-just-code-review/. ICP note: reframes the anxious executive's question from "how do I write a better prompt" to "how do I build a fast, trustworthy check on the output," a more teachable skill for non-technical leaders delegating to AI. Rung 4, domain prompting, strength signal.

3. **Agents are starting to reach out to humans on their own, and someone has to decide when.** Contrasts two models for autonomous agent factories: fully unreviewed autonomous work (StrongDM's "Software Factory") versus a facilitator agent that decides in real time when to loop a human in (the "Twilight Factory"). Source: Ethan Mollick, "Agency and Agents," One Useful Thing, 2026-08-31, https://www.oneusefulthing.org/p/agency-and-agents. ICP note: the practical shape of "human in the loop" CHROs will have to govern, a dynamic judgment call the agent itself makes, not a fixed approval gate. Rung 5, domain governance, strength signal.

4. **New research explains why long AI conversations degrade, and what fixes it.** A formal paper proves summarizing an agent's accumulated context is mathematically more efficient than selecting/dropping older parts, tested against Anthropic's real context-compaction feature. The field-wide takeaway: long chats fail because clutter makes the model reason worse even with room to spare, so the fix is well-timed summarization, not a bigger window. Source: arXiv 2608.01326, "Context Compaction Theory," submitted 2026-08-02, https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01326. ICP note: for any exec running very long Claude/ChatGPT sessions (a full strategy doc, a quarter of 1:1 notes), start fresh or use the tool's summarize/compact feature periodically. Rung 6, domain prompting, strength signal.

Four well-verified items chosen over padding to eight weaker ones.

## Sweep 3: Strategies and Frameworks (5 items)

1. **Gallup: AI's effect on workplace culture is a wash unless managers actively support it.** Among employees at AI-implemented organizations, 24% say culture improved, 25% say it worsened, 51% say no change. A companion Gallup Global CHRO Roundtable survey (n=102 CHROs, fielded 2026-02-10 to 2026-03-16) found 99% call AI "somewhat or very important" to strategy, but 50% are not confident in their own managers' ability to guide employees on AI use, and only 57% provide AI training for people managers. Source: Gallup, 2026-08-16, https://www.gallup.com/workplace/712976/ai-effect-workplace-culture.aspx. ICP note: usable proof that "AI strategy confidence" and "manager readiness" are two separate, mostly unsolved problems, even for CHROs who say the right things. Rung 2-3, domain ops/governance, strength signal.

2. **Colorado AG publishes draft ADMTA implementing rules.** Draft rules for the Automated Decision-Making Technology Act (SB 26-189, replacing the 2024 Colorado AI Act) published 2026-08-11; public comment runs through at least 2026-10-26. Underlying law (pre-use notice, 30-day adverse-outcome disclosure, 3-year record retention, human-review rights) takes effect 2027-01-01, this is the first look at operationalizing it, directly touching AI hiring/HR tools. Source: Davis Polk, 2026-08-13, https://www.davispolk.com/insights/client-update/colorado-repeals-and-replaces-landmark-ai-act-publishes-draft-rules-public. ICP note: CHROs using ADMT in hiring should start drafting the pre-use notice template now, not in December. Rung 3-4, domain regulation, strength signal. (Cross-tagged with Sweep 4, counted once.)

3. **McKinsey, "The State of AI" 2026 global survey.** n=1,719 executives and professionals. 37% attribute at least some EBIT impact to AI (flat versus 2025); only 6% qualify as "AI high performers" (5%+ EBIT impact, also flat year over year). 80% of individual AI users report productivity gains. 40% of $1B+ revenue organizations now scale AI agents, up from 27%. 39% expect AI-driven job cuts, up from 32% in 2025, though actual 2025 reductions fell well short of predictions. Source: secondary-corroborated (mckinsey.com blocked direct fetch twice): The Register, 2026-08-25, https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/08/25/mckinsey-says-enterprise-ai-is-finally-on-the-road-to-roi/5292388, matched by independent search aggregation. ICP note: the cleanest "value is stalling, not accelerating" counter to the anxious-exec narrative that everyone else has cracked ROI. Rung 3-5, domain ops, strength signal.

4. **Accenture, Pulse of Change 2026.** Two global surveys (3,000 C-suite leaders plus 3,000 employees, 20 countries, fielded April to June 2026). Share of organizations reporting "widespread, sustained business value from AI" fell from 32% to 23% during 2026 even as 82% of C-suite leaders keep increasing AI investment. Employee side: 81% feel AI increased their productivity, 71% report higher job satisfaction. Source: Accenture, 2026-07-26, https://www.accenture.com/us-en/insights/pulse-of-change. ICP note: value-realization falling while investment rises is a sharper, more contrarian talking point than "adoption is up." Rung 3-5, domain ops, strength signal.

Despite cost anxiety, 98% of investing leaders still report positive ROI. Source: EY, 2026-07-28, https://www.ey.com/en_us/newsroom/2026/07/ey-survey-c-suites-pivot-from-ai-adoption-to-unlocking-value-as-escalating-token-costs-trigger-fiscal-scrutiny. ICP note: gives a concrete reason finance is about to start scrutinizing HR tech AI budget lines, not just core engineering. Rung 4-6, domain ops, strength signal.

CLTR is formally asking the UK government for mandatory severe-incident reporting and emergency powers to compel AI-company information or temporarily restrict access. Source: CLTR, 2026-08-29, https://www.longtermresilience.org/reports/ai-loss-of-control-incidents-are-worsening-shows-cltr-analysis/.
