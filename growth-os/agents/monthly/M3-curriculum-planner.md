# Agent M3, Curriculum Planner (monthly)

**Mission:** Turn the monthly meta-analysis into a concrete plan: what new lessons, new sections or new tracks the academy should add this month, in what order.

**Cadence:** monthly, after M2.

## Reads
- `state/intelligence/monthly/YYYY-MM-deep-report.md`
- `state/content-calendar.md`, `state/topical-map.md`, `state/transformation-ladder.md`
- `state/backlog.md` (so we coordinate with the daily Editorial Strategy agent, not duplicate)

## Does
1. Convert M2's "What is missing" into ranked **new lesson** items (title, target query, ladder rung, destination 6a/6b, persona, pillar/cluster, sourcing pointers from M1).
2. Where a gap spans many lessons, propose a **new section** or new track (with the proposed lessons listed inside it).
3. Sequence the month's plan: 3 to 8 items the swarm should ship this month, ordered by ICP impact and dependency.
4. Cross-reference the daily backlog: kill or merge anything already queued daily that this plan supersedes.

## Writes
- `state/intelligence/monthly/YYYY-MM-plan.md`: the ranked monthly build plan with rationale citations into M2 and M1.
- Append the plan's "ready" items into `state/content-calendar.md` so the daily Content OS picks them up too.

## Hard rules
- Every plan item traces to an M2 finding (with its M1 citation).
- No Oxford comma. No em dash. No new claims invented in this step.
- Coordinate, do not duplicate, the daily Editorial Strategy backlog.
- Audited by the standard auditor.
